# TORUS — Outline Technical Specification

**AAI-2601 · Stage 3 · Rev P03**

Elemental, for coordination. Not a tender specification.

---

## 0 · Setting out

All geometry is set out from the centre of the torus at ground level.
Site datum **+0.000 = 42.600 AOD**.

| Symbol | Value | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `MAJOR_R` | 30 000 | Centre to tube centreline |
| `TUBE_R` | 7 500 | Radius of the swept section |
| `TUBE_Z` | +2 100 | Height of the tube axis |
| `ENV_T` | 350 | Envelope build-up thickness |
| θ | — | Plan angle, anticlockwise from east |
| φ | — | Section angle, 0° at the outermost point of the tube |

Derived, not to be re-entered by hand: springing radii 22 800 / 37 200;
widest 37 500 at +2 100; crown +9 600 at radius 30 000; slab edges
22 800 / 37 200 on both levels.

## 1 · Substructure

- 600 mm CFA piles, 18 m nominal, 148 no. Design working load 1 200 kN.
- Continuous RC ring beam 1 100 × 900 under each springing line, C40/50,
  two rings totalling 377 m.
- Pile caps to column positions on C2 and C4, 1 800 × 1 800 × 900.
- 200 mm RC ground-bearing slab, C32/40, on 150 mm PIR on DPM on 150 mm
  blinding. U-value 0.15 W/m²K.
- Movement joints at R09, R18, R27, R36 through the slab only.
- Radon barrier: not required, low-risk area. Gas membrane omitted.

- Grade S355 throughout. Galvanised to EN ISO 1461, factory finish.

- Stability: braced bays within each of the four cores, one per core,
  between C2 and C4.
- Composite floor: 130 mm LC on 60 mm re-entrant deck, 300 mm overall,
  A252 mesh, through-deck welded studs.

## 3 · Envelope

### 3.1 Curved glazing (GL zones)

- Unit: 8 mm toughened outer / 16 mm argon cavity / 6.8 mm laminated inner,
  soft-coat low-e on face 3. Ug 1.1 W/m²K, g-value 0.28, light
  transmittance 0.66.
- **Cold-bent to a single radius of 7 500 about the tube axis.** No unit is
  doubly curved.
- Mullions: extruded aluminium on the radial grid at 2.5° centres —
  1 636 mm at the widest circle, 982 mm at the courtyard face. Polyamide
  thermal break, psi 0.08 W/m·K at the base shoe.
- Every third bay on the courtyard face and at both springing lines carries
  an actuated opening vent for night purge.
- Base shoe cast into an upstand; 30 mm structural thermal break pad under.

### 3.2 Crown (CR zones)

- 0.9 mm aluminium standing seam, 400 mm bays, on 200 mm rigid insulation
  on a steel liner tray with vapour control layer. U 0.13 W/m²K.
- φ 64° to 88°: photovoltaic laminate bonded to the seam, 628 m²,
  estimated 118 MWh/yr.
- φ 90° to 118°: eight rooflight slots, triple glazed, argon filled,
  178 m² total, Ug 1.30 W/m²K.
- Perforated acoustic soffit lining below the liner tray.

### 3.3 Rainwater

- Two continuous ring gutters at the springing lines, 300 × 300 aluminium,
  grated at the outer face, leaf-guarded at the courtyard.
- 72 outlets at 10° centres to attenuation beneath the courtyard.
- Harvested for irrigation and WC flushing.

## 4 · Internal finishes

- Raised access floor 150 mm on both levels, 600 grid, medium grade.
- Services and ceiling zone 350 mm below the Level 01 slab; perforated metal
  ceiling to the occupied band, exposed soffit over the loop.
- Level 01 has no flat ceiling over the central 8 m of the plate: the shell
  lining is the ceiling, acoustically treated.
- Cores: fair-faced blockwork, painted; terrazzo to WC floors.
- Loop: linear timber flooring, 188 m continuous.

## 5 · Services

- Ground-source heat pumps, 48 boreholes at 140 m under the courtyard,
  seasonal COP 4.1. Four-pipe fan coils in the perimeter zone, chilled
  beams to the deep zone.
- Mixed mode: BMS opens the springing-line vents between 12 °C and 22 °C
  external, with night purge from 02:00.
- Risers in each of the four cores; horizontal distribution radially from
  the core, so no run exceeds a quarter of the ring.
- Lighting: 300 lux general, daylight-linked dimming on every perimeter
  row. Installed load 5.5 W/m².
- Two 13-person lifts per core, 1 100 × 2 100 car, 1.6 m/s.

## 6 · Performance targets

| | Target |
|---|---|
| Operational energy | 55 kWh/m²/yr including small power |
| Air permeability | 3.0 m³/h·m² at 50 Pa |
| Embodied carbon (A1–A5) | < 550 kgCO₂e/m² |
| Daylight, spatial autonomy | > 65 % of the occupied band at 300 lux |
| Overheating | TM52 compliant without mechanical cooling in the loop |

## 7 · Fire

- Purpose group 3, offices. Two storeys; top storey +4.200.
- Four protected stairs, 1 400 wide, direct discharge to open air.
- Maximum travel to a protected stair 23.6 m, escape in two directions.
- Occupancy 657 at 1:8; stair capacity 880 with one stair discounted.
- 60 minute compartments: each core, the plant zone, the switchroom.
- Sprinklers throughout, tank and pump in the northern plant zone.
- Dry riser inlets at each core, all within 45 m of hardstanding.
- The northern undercroft is treated as external.
