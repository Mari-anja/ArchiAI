The parti

The building is a torus, not a ring in plan

A circle of radius 7 500 swept around a vertical axis at radius 30 000, with its axis at +2.100 so the ground plane truncates it exactly at the ground-floor slab. The shell springs straight out of the paving on two concentric circles, leans outward to its widest at chest height, and returns to a crown 9.6 m up. There is no plinth, no base course and no ramp anywhere on the approach: the external ground plane and FFL 00 share one datum all the way round.

P(θ, φ) = ( (30 000 + 7 500·cos φ)·cos θ, (30 000 + 7 500·cos φ)·sin θ, 2 100 + 7 500·sin φ )

Two angles describe every point on the building, and every drawing in the set is annotated in them. θ is the plan angle, anticlockwise from east, with radial gridlines R01–R36 at 10° centres. φ is the section angle within the tube: 0° at the outermost point, 90° at the crown, 180° facing the courtyard.

Two plates

Both floors came out identical, and that is the argument

Both floors are horizontal chords of the same generating circle, placed symmetrically about its axis at −2.100 and +2.100. Symmetric chords are equal chords, so both plates are annuli from radius 22 800 to 37 200 — 14 400 deep, 2 714 m² each.

That depth is the whole case for the building type. A 14.4 m plate glazed on both faces puts no desk more than 7.2 m from a window and opens the floor to cross ventilation across its full depth. A conventional deep plan of the same area would be lit from one side and mechanically ventilated throughout.

The geometry is paid for on Level 01, where the shell curves down to meet the slab. Head height falls below 2 100 outside radius 36 214 and inside radius 23 786 — about a metre at each edge, 373 m² in total. That strip takes perimeter services, low storage and window seating, and it is where the curved soffit meets the glass, which is the best thing about being on that floor.

Three concentric bands

22 800–26 000 is the loop: a continuous 3 200 wide circulation ring on the courtyard side, glazed to the court for its whole 188 m length. 26 000–37 200 is the occupied band. Four cores at 45°, 135°, 225° and 315° plug that band completely and take the outer face. Putting the cores against the outer facade and the circulation against the courtyard is the reverse of the usual arrangement, and it is what makes the loop worth walking.

Two sheets are worth singling out: A-300, because a plane through the axis of a torus cuts it in two true circles, and A-202, because for a building with no flat facade the unrolled elevation is the only one that tells you where anything is. Model and source

Everything comes from one file of parameters

Change MAJOR_R or TUBE_Z in params.py and every plan, section, elevation, schedule, mesh and viewer regenerates consistently — including the derived figures written into the drawing notes. Pure Python, standard library only; the whole set redraws in about six tenths of a second.

torus-office.obj

Wavefront mesh, 25 182 faces in 17 named groups, metres. Opens in Blender, Rhino, SketchUp or MeshLab.

torus-office.scad

Parametric OpenSCAD source with customiser parameters and a built-in cutaway toggle.

viewer.html

The model above, as a standalone file. No network, no libraries, no build step.

src/archiai/

The generator: params, programme, an ISO drafting kit that writes SVG, and a painter's-algorithm renderer for the axonometrics.

Stage 3 issue

What is still open

Stated plainly, because they are real.

Access

Undercroft headroom

The northern gateway gives 3 550 clear, which suits service vehicles but is below the 3 700 usually wanted for a fire appliance. The strategy currently relies on perimeter access and a courtyard hydrant instead. If the authority requires appliance access into the court, the Level 01 slab has to be locally raised or the gateway widened.

Fabrication

Cold-bending tolerance

The 7 500 radius is a comfortable cold bend for the outer units but tight for the laminated inner leaf at full panel size. This needs a specialist review before the panel module is fixed.

Brief

The Level 01 perimeter

373 m² sits below 2 100 head height. It is programmed as services and seating, but it should be tested against the client's own space standards rather than assumed away.

Maintenance

Cleaning the shell

A crown walkway is shown; the flanks below +6.000 need either a mast climber running on the perimeter road or a rope access strategy. Not yet resolved.

Acoustics

A concave courtyard

A 45 m circular court will focus sound. The inner facade should carry diffusing relief, or the planting should be denser than currently shown.
